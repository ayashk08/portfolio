## ⚡ QUICK START (FOLLOW IN ORDER)

### STEP 1️⃣ : CREATE LOGO (20-30 minutes)

**Choose ONE method:**

**Option A: DIY with Canva (RECOMMENDED)**
1. Go to: https://canva.com
2. Create → Logo template
3. Search: "tech developer logo" or "programmer logo"
4. Customize with:
   - Your initials "AS" or name "Ayashkanta"
   - Professional colors (blue, green, or purple)
   - Tech elements (code brackets, circuits, etc.)
5. Export as PNG (transparent background) → 512x512
6. Save as: `e:\portfolio\assets\logo\logo-main.png`

**Option B: AI Generator (10 minutes)**
1. Go to: https://looka.com
2. Enter: "Ayashkanta" + "Software Developer"
3. Choose from 100+ AI-generated logos
4. Download best version
5. Save as: `e:\portfolio\assets\logo\logo-main.png`

**Option C: Hire Designer ($20-50, 3-7 days)**
1. Go to: Fiverr.com or 99designs.com
2. Search: "minimalist tech logo"
3. Negotiate price and timeline
4. Save received logo as: `e:\portfolio\assets\logo\logo-main.png`

✅ **After completing:** Copy logo file also to `logo-dark.png` (same image)

### STEP 2️⃣ : GET PROFESSIONAL PHOTO (1-3 hours or 3-7 days)

**Choose ONE method:**

**Option A: Professional Photographer (BEST QUALITY)**
1. Search local photographers on Google Maps or Yelp
2. Book 30-minute session
3. Wear business casual attire
4. Use neutral background
5. Take 10-20 shots, pick the best
6. Get digital files

**Option B: DIY with Smartphone (FREE)**
1. Find well-lit location (near window)
2. Use plain background (white wall or blue backdrop)
3. Wear professional/business casual
4. Smile naturally at camera
5. Take 20+ photos using portrait mode
6. Pick the clearest one with best lighting

**Option C: AI Service (QUICK & PROFESSIONAL)**
1. Go to: https://headshotpro.com or photobomb.com
2. Upload regular photo or selfie
3. AI generates professional headshot
4. Download result
5. Takes 5-10 minutes

✅ **After getting photo:**
1. Crop to square (400x400 pixels)
2. Slightly blur background (optional)
3. Save as: `e:\portfolio\assets\photos\profile-photo.jpg`
