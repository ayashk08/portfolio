### PHASE 2: CREATE YOUR LOGO (3 Options)

#### Option A: DIY Logo Design (Recommended - Free)
**Tools:** Canva Pro (Free trial) or Figma

1. Go to **canva.com** or **figma.com**
2. Create new design → "Logo"
3. Choose template related to tech/development
4. Customize with:
   - **Name:** Ayashkanta Sahoo / AS
   - **Initials:** AS in stylized form
   - **Colors:** Professional blues, greens, or purples
5. Export as PNG (transparent background) at 512x512
6. Save to `e:\portfolio\assets\logo\`

#### Option B: AI Logo Generator (5 minutes)
- **looka.com** - AI creates 100+ logo options
- **logojoy.com** - Quick brand identity
- **brandmark.io** - Professional logos

Steps:
1. Enter your name and industry (Software Development)
2. AI generates logos
3. Download preferred logo
4. Save as `logo-main.png` and `logo-dark.png`

#### Option C: Hire Designer (Budget: $20-50)
- **fiverr.com** - Search "minimalist tech logo"
- **99designs.com** - Logo contest
- **upwork.com** - Freelance designers

### PHASE 3: PROFESSIONAL PHOTO (Required)

#### Getting Your Professional Headshot

**Option A: Professional Photographer (~$50-200)**
- Schedule 30-min session
- Neutral background (preferred)
- Business casual attire
- Get 5-10 photos

**Option B: DIY Photography**
1. Use good lighting (natural window light)
2. Plain background (white, gray, or light blue)
3. Wear business casual
4. Use phone camera with portrait mode
5. Take 20+ photos, pick best

**Option C: Online Service (~$20-50)**
- **headshotpro.com** - AI professional photos
- **photobomb.com** - Quick professional photos

**After Getting Photo:**
1. Crop to square (400x400 pixels)
2. Slightly blur background
3. Save as `profile-photo.jpg`
4. Place in `e:\portfolio\assets\photos\`
