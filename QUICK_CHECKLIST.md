## 🎨 DESIGN TIPS FOR LOGO

### What Makes a Good Logo?
✅ **Simple** - No more than 2-3 elements  
✅ **Memorable** - Easy to remember  
✅ **Scalable** - Works small and large  
✅ **Versatile** - Works in color and B&W  
✅ **Professional** - Reflects your field  

### Logo Ideas for Developers
- Your initials in modern font (AS)
- Code bracket symbols < >
- Combination of initials + tech element
- Abstract geometric shapes
- Your first/last name in unique font

### Color Recommendations
- **Primary:** Blue (#0066CC) or Green (#00AA44)
- **Secondary:** Purple (#7C3AED) or Orange (#FF6B35)
- **Professional:** Dark Gray (#333333)
- **Neutral:** White/Light Gray for backgrounds

## 📸 TIPS FOR PROFESSIONAL PHOTO

### DIY Photo Guide
1. **Location:** Near window (natural light)
2. **Background:** Wall - plain color (white, gray, or blue)
3. **Clothing:** Business casual, solid color
4. **Expression:** Genuine smile, eyes looking at camera
5. **Camera:** Phone portrait mode, 3-4 feet distance
6. **Lighting:** Test 20+ shots, pick the best

### Professional Services
- **Prices:** $50-150 for professional session
- **Includes:** 5-10 edited photos
- **Duration:** 30-60 minutes
- **Best Cities:** Find local photographers on Yelp

### AI Photo Service
- **Cost:** $20-50
- **Time:** 5 minutes
- **Quality:** Professional-looking
- **Website:** headshotpro.com, photobomb.com
